The Problem

Civic issues go unreported — or unresolved.

Every city has potholes, flooded drains, and electrical hazards that linger for weeks because there's no fast, verifiable way for citizens to report them and no transparent system for officials to track and close them out.

Slow reporting

Existing channels require lengthy form submissions, phone calls, or visits to municipal offices — deterring most citizens from ever filing a report.

No transparency

Citizens have no visibility into whether their reports are being acted upon, leading to frustration, duplicate submissions, and eroded public trust.

Inefficient prioritisation

Officials receive unstructured reports with no categorisation, no location accuracy, and no signal about which issues are most urgently affecting the community.

Our Solution

CivicSync puts a camera, GPS, and AI triage pipeline in every citizen's pocket. One tap, one photo, and the platform handles the rest — from classification to real-time status updates visible to the whole community.

How It Works

From photo to resolution in minutes.

  1. 01

    Capture & locate

    Open the app, tap "Report Issue", and take a live photo with your camera. GPS coordinates are captured automatically to within 50 metres.

  2. 02

    AI triage

    Gemini 2.5 Flash analyses the image and location, generating a category, title, and description. Review and confirm in seconds — or edit as needed.

  3. 03

    Community upvoting

    Other citizens can upvote existing reports near them instead of filing duplicates, surfacing the most critical issues to the top of the official queue.

  4. 04

    Official resolution

    Government officials use the Kanban dashboard to move issues from "To Do" through "In Progress" to "Done". Status changes propagate to all citizens in real time.

Technology Stack

Built entirely on Google technologies.

Every layer of CivicSync — from the mobile UI to the AI triage engine — runs on Google's cloud-native platform.

Flutter

Cross-platform Android app with camera capture, GPS, real-time Firestore listeners, and role-based UI morphing for citizens and officials.

Mobile Client

Go on Cloud Run

Single self-contained Go binary deployed serverlessly. Handles authentication, AI triage, geospatial deduplication, ticket lifecycle, and static serving.

Backend & API

Google OAuth

Password-free sign-in via Google Identity Services. ID tokens verified against Google's public key infrastructure on every API request.

Authentication

Cloud Firestore

NoSQL document database that stores all user profiles and civic tickets. Real-time snapshot listeners push status updates to citizens within 3 seconds.

Database & Real-time

Cloud Storage for Firebase

Stores all citizen-captured issue images and hosts the downloadable Android APK. Directly accessible from the Flutter app and the Go backend.

Object Storage

Gemini 2.5 Flash

Multimodal AI model that classifies civic issue images into structured categories, titles, and descriptions — eliminating manual data entry for citizens.
